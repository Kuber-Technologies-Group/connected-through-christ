# Product Vision — Connected Through Christ Platform

**Project:** Christian E-Commerce & Community Platform  
**Client:** Connected Through Christ (CTC)  
**Prepared by:** Kuber Technologies  
**Last Updated:** May 2026  

---

## Mission Statement

To give the Connected Through Christ ministry a permanent, professional digital home where the community can grow spiritually, purchase faith-based products, ask questions rooted in Scripture, and connect with one another — all in one place.

---

## The Problem We Are Solving

Right now, CTC has no central online presence. This means:

- Members have no single place to find and buy CTC products
- There is no consistent way to deliver daily spiritual encouragement to the community
- Faith questions go unanswered or are scattered across social media
- Christian brands have no faith-aligned platform to advertise on
- CTC has no passive income stream to sustain and grow the ministry

---

## The Solution

A purpose-built web platform that brings **faith, community, and commerce** together in one place — designed to reflect the values of CTC and serve real people at every stage of their spiritual journey.

---

## Who This Is For

| Audience | Their Need |
|---|---|
| CTC community members | Daily spiritual encouragement and a place to buy faith products |
| New visitors | An introduction to the CTC ministry and its values |
| Youth & adults with Bible questions | A trusted, moderated space to ask and learn |
| Christian business owners & ministries | A faith-aligned audience to advertise to |

---

## What Success Looks Like (6-Month View)

By the end of Phase 2 (approximately 5–7 weeks from project start), success means:

- [ ] The platform is live and accessible from any device
- [ ] CTC products are browsable and enquiries can be made
- [ ] A daily verse is published every day without developer involvement
- [ ] The CTC admin team can update all content independently
- [ ] At least one Christian brand has an advert running on the platform

By the end of Phase 4 (approximately 3–5 months), success means:

- [ ] Customers can pay for products directly on the website
- [ ] The Bible Q&A section is live with at least 20 published answers
- [ ] The platform is generating income from both product sales and brand advertising
- [ ] Community members are returning to the site regularly

---

## Core Values That Must Be Reflected in Every Design Decision

1. **Faith-first** — Every feature must serve the spiritual mission, not undermine it
2. **Accessible** — Works beautifully on mobile phones (most users will be on mobile)
3. **Trust** — The platform must feel safe, professional, and biblically sound
4. **Community** — People should feel like they belong here, not like they're shopping at a generic store
5. **Simplicity** — Easy for CTC admin to manage without calling a developer

---

## What This Platform Is NOT

- It is not a social media platform (no public profiles, no follower counts)
- It is not a general Christian marketplace (CTC controls who advertises)
- It is not a sermon or church management platform
- It does not replace pastoral care — the Q&A is for guidance, not counselling

---

## Platform Personality

If Connected Through Christ were a person, they would be:

> Warm, grounded, knowledgeable about Scripture, approachable to young people, and deeply invested in the community's growth.

The platform's design, copy, and interactions should reflect this personality at every touchpoint.
